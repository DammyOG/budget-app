TRANSACTION CATEGORIZATION
================================================================================

INCOME vs. EXPENSES
  - Categories have "isIncome" boolean flag (only "Income" category marked true)
  - Amount Convention (Plaid):
    * Positive = Money OUT (expenses/charges)
    * Negative = Money IN (income/deposits)

DEFAULT CATEGORIES (17 total):
  Expenses: Groceries, Dining & Restaurants, Rent & Mortgage, Utilities,
            Transportation, Shopping, Entertainment, Health & Fitness, Travel,
            Subscriptions, Insurance, Personal Care, Education, Gifts & Donations,
            Fees & Charges, Transfer
  Income:   Income (with isIncome=true)

KEY FILTERING RULES:
  - Budgets page: .filter((c) => !c.isIncome) → only expense categories
  - Dashboard: amount: { gt: 0 } → only positive amounts (excludes income)

DATABASE SCHEMA HIGHLIGHTS
================================================================================

TRANSACTION
  - id, plaidTransactionId, accountId, categoryId
  - amount (positive=out, negative=in), date, name, merchantName
  - pending, isManual, notes
  - Indexes: accountId, categoryId, date
  
CATEGORY
  - id, name (unique), icon, isIncome, createdAt
  
ACCOUNT
  - id, plaidItemId, plaidAccountId
  - name, institutionName, type, subtype, mask
  - currentBalance, isManual, archivedAt
  
BUDGET
  - id, categoryId, month (YYYY-MM), amount
  - Unique constraint: (categoryId, month)

DATABASE: SQLite (swappable via DATABASE_URL env var)
